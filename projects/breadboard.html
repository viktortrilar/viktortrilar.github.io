Breadboard circuit checker

NIT Sendai, Japan, 2026

Students photograph their breadboard with a phone and get told whether it’s wired correctly. YOLO finds the components, a heading-aware reinforcement learning agent follows each wire, and the result is compared with the circuit the teacher set. The models were trained on synthetic images rendered in Blender, and the whole thing runs as a Japanese and English FastAPI web app. Built during a research exchange at NIT Sendai under a JSPS grant.

  • Read the technical report (PDF)

Results

99.4%wires matched on 3,000 synthetic images
99.5%mAP for component detection
100%on real boards with distinct wire colours
87%board-level blind discovery

How it works

  1. Find the board

    Locate the breadboard in the photo and straighten it into a standard view.

  2. Separate the wires

    Segment the wires from the board and components so only the conductive paths remain.

  3. Trace each wire

    A heading-aware deep reinforcement learning agent follows every wire from one end to the other across the mask.

  4. Detect the components

    YOLO finds and classifies each component and the breadboard holes its pins sit in.

  5. Check the circuit

    Rebuild the netlist from the traced wires and detected components, then compare it with the expected circuit.
